Six symbol-led styles for the new site

Every option uses a minimal palette (one neutral, one ink, at most one accent) and a set of symbols for decoration. Each preview uses the copy and WhatsApp links from borrador 1. Only the look changes. Por qué and Quiénes somos are left out of the previews to keep them short.

Brief asked for
Minimal colour, symbols as decoration, imagery like the Om, lotus emblem and line icon references
Rule
No people in yoga poses. Only the seated lotus figure and the lotus flower
Included
Only styles that already use this kind of imagery

Shared symbol set

Drawn from your references. Each style redraws the same symbols in its own line weight or fill. Every symbol stands for something on the page.

Rules

  • ✓Lotus flower and the seated lotus figure (sukhasana / padmasana). Nothing else with a human body.
  • ✕Standing or moving poses, like the tree-pose figure with arrows in the line-icon set, and any other asana silhouette.
  • !Om is a sacred symbol in Hinduism, Buddhism and Jainism. Use it once, large and upright. Never as a bullet, pattern, cropped or rotated.
  • !Third eye and hamsa read as esoteric to an HR buyer. They are left out of all six styles.
  • ✓Plan cards show a lotus with 1, 3 or 6 petals, one per activity in the plan.

Styles left out

  • Glassmorphism and soft 3D icons. The current icon trend, but built on gradients and depth rather than symbols.
  • Neo-brutalism. Heavy borders and loud colour clash with a calm, minimal palette, and it has no symbol tradition.
  • Organic blobs and gradients. Common on wellness sites, but abstract. Symbols would have to be bolted on.
  • Swiss / editorial grid. Very clear for HR, but it uses type and photos, not symbols.

Recommendation

Start from Línea serena. It keeps the brand green, its icons do real work (an HR reader scans benefits and activities by icon) and it reads the most corporate. Two ideas from other options carry over well: the moon phases for the four steps (Noche celeste) and the petal-count lotus on the plans. Keep Om out of the site until Carolyn approves it.
